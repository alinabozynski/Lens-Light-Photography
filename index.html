A little about the artist

Hello, I'm Jane

Lifestyle, Portrait, and Wedding Photographer

I'm a photographer drawn to the honest, in-between moments—the quiet details, genuine connections, and little pieces of a story that might otherwise go unnoticed.

Photography, to me, is more than taking a beautiful picture. It's about preserving how a moment felt. I focus on natural interactions, genuine emotion, and the little details that make your story uniquely yours.

Take a look around, and thanks for being here. I'd love to tell your story.
